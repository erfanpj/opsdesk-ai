## HB-DEF-01 | Warranty claims
keywords: warranty, defect, defective, stopped working, faulty, broke after, keeps slipping, creaking, battery won't charge, battery does not charge
action: create_warranty_case
Parts and e-bike batteries have a two-year warranty against manufacturing defects. Agents open a warranty case with the order number and a short description of the fault.
