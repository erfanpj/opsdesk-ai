## HB-DMG-01 | Damaged on arrival
keywords: damaged, broken on arrival, arrived broken, scratched, bent, crushed box, dented
action: request_damage_photos
For items damaged in transit, first ask the customer for photos of the item and the packaging. Do not promise a replacement before the photos are reviewed.

## HB-DMG-02 | Replacement after photo review
keywords: photos attached, sent the photos, pictures attached, here are the photos
action: create_replacement_order
Once the customer has sent photos showing transport damage, agents may create a free replacement order for the damaged item.
