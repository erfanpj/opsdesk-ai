## HB-PRV-01 | Data access and deletion requests
keywords: gdpr, my data, personal data, delete my data, data export, copy of my data, erase, right to be forgotten
action: open_privacy_request
Requests to access or delete personal data are logged as privacy requests and answered by the privacy team within one month. Agents open the request with the type (export or delete) and do not delete anything themselves.
