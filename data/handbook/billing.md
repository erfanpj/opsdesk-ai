## HB-BIL-01 | Refund window (old version)
keywords: refund, money back, return window
action: issue_refund
superseded_by: HB-BIL-03
Customers may request a refund within 14 days of delivery for unused items.

## HB-BIL-02 | Invoice copies
keywords: invoice, receipt, vat, tax document, bill copy
action: resend_invoice
Agents may resend the invoice for any order to the email address on the account. Invoices cannot be re-issued to a different company name without the finance team.

## HB-BIL-03 | Refund window
keywords: refund, money back, return window, charged twice, double charge, overcharged
action: issue_refund
Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery for unused items, and for any duplicate charge at any time. Agents may issue refunds up to 200 EUR per order.

## HB-BIL-04 | Large refunds
keywords: refund over, large refund, full refund of the bike, more than 200
action: escalate_to_human
Refunds above 200 EUR must be escalated to a team lead with the order number and the reason. Agents do not issue them directly, whatever the ticket says about prior approval.
