## HB-SUB-01 | Changing the care plan
keywords: upgrade, downgrade, change plan, switch plan, care plan, basic plan, plus plan, pro plan
action: change_plan
The bike-care subscription has three plans: Basic, Plus and Pro. Agents may change the plan on request; the new price applies from the next billing month.

## HB-SUB-02 | Cancelling the subscription
keywords: cancel, cancellation, stop my subscription, end my subscription, unsubscribe from the plan
action: cancel_subscription
Customers can cancel the care subscription at any time with effect from the end of the current billing month. Agents confirm the end date in their reply.
