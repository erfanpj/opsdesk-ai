## HB-PRI-01 | Setting ticket urgency
keywords: urgency, priority
action:
Critical: suspected account takeover, or an outage that stops customers from ordering. High: privacy requests, locked-out customers, items damaged on arrival and lost parcels. Low: invoice copies and subscription plan changes. Everything else is normal.
